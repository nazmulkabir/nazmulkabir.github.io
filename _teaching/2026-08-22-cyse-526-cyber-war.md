Instructor, Fall 2026. Graduate course (3 credit hours), cross-listed with CYSE 426.

From the ODU catalog: the course explores the national security dimensions of cybersecurity
and examines cyber war in international relations. It begins with cybersecurity as a component
of national security and investigates U.S. national cybersecurity and other national approaches
to cyber war. Topics include cyber deterrence, cyber as a military domain, the roles of
international organizations in cyber war, cyber terrorism, the role of social media, information
warfare, and the international dimension of cybersecurity.
