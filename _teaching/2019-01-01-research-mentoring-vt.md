Mentored graduate students on experimental design, reproducible ML practice, and scientific
writing in secure AI for cyber-physical systems.

Mentees include **Trey Ward** and **Shubham Deshmukh** (M.S. students, Virginia Tech),
co-mentored on *Unified Detection and Attribution of Synthetic and Adversarial Images in
Agricultural Cyber-Physical Systems* — a multi-generator evaluation framework spanning GANs
(StyleGAN2, StyleGAN3, R3GAN) and diffusion models (InstructPix2Pix, BLIP-Diffusion,
Dreamshaper-8) across apple, maize, and tomato, using EfficientNet-B0, ResNet-50, and CLIP
backbones for health-state classification, source detection, and generator attribution.
